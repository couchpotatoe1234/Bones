# Bones
Bones is a rather simple platformer game created in the Godot Game engine. You start as a skull and collect coins to attempt to 'buy' back the rest of your body. You then gain a double jump and continue the parkour. 

## How it was made
All of the levels were designed by hand as was all the art, sounds, music, and code. The pixel art was drawn in Godot's extension, PixelPen. Sound was recorded on a phone and converted to MP3 and the music was made in MuseScore and exported from there.  The rest of the stuff was made in Godot.
